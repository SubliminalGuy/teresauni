Our Mission

A Dedication to Scholarly Excellence and Discovery

Founded in 1947, Teresais University was built on the conviction that understanding the past is an act of civic responsibility. Our scholars do not merely study history — they excavate it, argue with it, and bring it into dialogue with the present.

From Bronze Age settlements in the Levant to the archival records of colonial Spain, our faculty and students work across every dimension of the human experience, guided by rigorous method and genuine intellectual curiosity.

Research Spotlight

Advancing the Frontiers of Knowledge

Our research centres are engaged in landmark projects that are rewriting the scholarly consensus across the globe.

🏛
Archaeology

The Iberian Bronze Age Project

Led by Dr. Teresa Serrano Martín, this multi-year excavation at the Serra d'Atapuerca sites is revealing new evidence of early urban settlement patterns in pre-Roman Hispania, challenging long-held chronological models.

📜
History

Colonial Archives & Subaltern Voices

A collaborative project digitising 15th–18th century colonial documents from Spain, Portugal, and the Americas, making thousands of primary sources freely available to scholars worldwide.

🌍
Archaeometry

Isotope Mapping of Ancient Trade Routes

Using strontium and lead isotope analysis of artefacts recovered across the Mediterranean, our team is constructing a comprehensive map of ancient commercial and cultural exchange networks.

🗺
Digital Humanities

The Roman Cadastre Reconstruction Project

Combining LiDAR survey data with GIS modelling to digitally reconstruct Roman land-survey systems across southern Europe, preserving an endangered cultural landscape for future study.

👩‍🔬
Dr. Teresa Serrano Martín
Director & Chair of Archaeology
